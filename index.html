~ Instructions ~

1. Click the button to deal four cards.
2. If there are two cards of the same suit, click on the one with the lower rank to discard. Aces rank high.
3. Repeat until there's no more than one card of each suit.
4. Repeat steps 1-3 until either:
a) all the aces are on top. You win!
b) you run through the whole deck without getting all the aces face up on top of the card stacks. You lose.

Refresh the page to start a new game.
